A simulation, not a fact. The remaining schedule (Weeks 2 to 14) was played 10,000 times; each week every team's score was drawn at random around its own average with its own swing, and the top 7 by wins, ties broken by points for (Sleeper's playoff_seed_type 0, its standard order: record, then points), made the playoffs. With fewer than 3 saved weeks each team's average and swing are pulled toward the league's: with 1 week, one third team and two thirds league (and the league's swing, since one week has none); with 2 weeks, two thirds team and one third league; from 3 weeks on, the team's own. The same saved data always gives the same odds.

Projection accuracy from week 2

Projections are saved before each week's first kickoff, starting with Week 2, so this table fills in once Week 2 is final. Week 1 has no saved projection and is not counted.

Projected is the total the board shows for the team's starters before the week's first kickoff (Sleeper's projected stats scored with this league's settings), saved once and never changed; actual is the final score. Beat it counts the weeks the team scored more than its projection.

Every number comes from files saved each week: each week's final points once all its games were final, that week's saved lineups, the schedule Sleeper lists, and the projections saved before kickoff. Playoff odds are a simulation and say so. Nothing is typed in by hand.
